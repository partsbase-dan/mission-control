Career Runway Sequences

Selling Career Runway exhibit space to employers. The evergreen sequence is the one to ship: email only, rolling enrolment, and it needs a single input. The four tiered sequences below add rep-led call and LinkedIn steps for when there is capacity to work the top accounts 1:1.

PBExpo 2027 · March 10–11, 2027 · Miami Beach Convention Center · 360 companies · 698 contacts · 590 with email
The evergreen sequence ships on one input. It needs {{SENDER}} and nothing else — {{BOOTH_URL}} appears once in the breakup, and the rest are written around. The four tiered sequences below need more. Tokens in orange are deliberate blanks, not oversights.
  • {{BOOTH_URL}} — Career Runway booking / enquiry page URL, with UTMs from the UTM registry. The evergreen sequence uses it once, in the breakup email only.
  • {{SENDER}} — Sending identity and reply-to. The only hard blocker on the evergreen sequence. Use a named person — four of the five emails ask for a reply, so it must land in a monitored inbox.
  • {{PRICE}} — Stand price and package contents. Tier 2 and Tier 4 only. The evergreen sequence is written around it.
  • {{DEADLINE}} — Space-reservation or early-rate cutoff. Tiered sequences only. The evergreen sequence is written around it.
  • {{PROOF_POINT}} — One verifiable 2026 Career Runway result. Tiered sequences only. Every slot is deliberately empty: no sequence ships with an invented statistic.
  • {{cr_open_roles}} — Verified open-role count. Florida list only, so the evergreen sequence does not use it.

Any employer hiring in Florida aviation. Rolling enrolment, no batch, no wave. Add contacts as they are enriched or as new lists land.

Motion. Email only, five steps over 26 days, timed from the day each contact is enrolled rather than from the event. No call or LinkedIn tasks, so it needs no rep capacity. Unenrols on reply.
Why this copy. Written against the cold-email playbook, not the marketing-email one. Subject lines are two or three lowercase words that look like internal mail rather than a pitch. Four of the five emails ask for a one-line reply instead of a click, which converts better cold and keeps link count down while sender reputation is still being rebuilt. Every email opens in the reader's world and stands alone, because a rolling enrolment means nobody is guaranteed to have read the previous one.
What makes it evergreen
  • Timing is relative to enrolment day, not to March 10. No email says "next week" or "two weeks out".
  • No {{cr_open_roles}} token. That data exists for the Florida list only, and an empty token in a cold email is worse than no personalisation. Tier 1 contacts who deserve that specificity should run the rep-led sequence instead.
  • One link, in the last email only. Cold email with multiple links draws spam filtering, and PartsBase sender reputation is still being rebuilt. The other four ask for a reply.
  • Ships on one input. Only {{SENDER}} is required — {{BOOTH_URL}} appears once, in the breakup, and {{PRICE}}, {{DEADLINE}} and {{PROOF_POINT}} are written around entirely.
  • Stop enrolling on 2027-02-05. The sequence runs 26 days and the last email needs to land with real time before the March 10 doors.
