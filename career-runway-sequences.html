What makes these evergreen
  • Every step is timed from the day a contact is enrolled, not from March 10. No email says "next week" or "two weeks out", so a person added in November reads the same arc as one added in January.
  • Email only. No call or LinkedIn steps in any of the four, so none of this needs rep capacity.
  • One link per sequence, in the breakup email only. Cold email with multiple links draws spam filtering, and PartsBase sender reputation is still being rebuilt after the Mailchimp remediation. Every other email asks for a one-line reply.
  • {{cr_open_roles}} appears in Tier 1 and Tier 2 only, where coverage is 95% and 93%. Set the HubSpot fallback to "several" so the remainder still read naturally. Tier 3 has zero coverage and Tier 4 has 24% — the token is absent from both.
