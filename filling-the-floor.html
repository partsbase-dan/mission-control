Lead hook — the #1 message
“Walk in with a schedule full of leads.”
Every channel leads with PBExpo’s AI-powered matchmaking: the app identifies attendees with matching intent, so buyers pre-book best-fit meetings instead of wandering the floor. It reframes the pitch from “attend a show” to “bring your team a pipeline” — and it is the same promise the paid ads already carry.
The three tracks
Track A · Buyers + operators Primary
New and cold registrations. MROs, distributors, brokers, OEMs, contract manufacturers, plus airlines and the Operators Pavilion. Paid is live here now — two LinkedIn video ads running against free registration.
Track B · Past attendees Warm
Prior-year registrants — the highest-intent, lowest-cost pool we have, and Amelia shows returning attendees now outnumber first-timers. No dedicated flow is scheduled. This is the single largest unworked source of registrations in the window.
Track C · Employers hiring Career Runway
Aviation companies with open roles — the side that buys Career Runway space. Revenue rather than attendance, but every hiring booth also brings a staffed team onto the floor. Four sequences are written and waiting.

The September action is consent, not sending. We already collect a mobile number at registration — 34 of the 38 rows in the August export have one — but there is no opt-in field on the form. Without recorded consent those numbers cannot be texted. Adding the checkbox now means the list is usable by February; adding it in February means starting from zero.

What would unlock more
A named sender for Career Runway
A person with a real reply-to, not a marketing alias. It is the only hard blocker across all four sequences — everything else is written.
The prior-year attendee list
Track B is the cheapest registrations available and has nothing scheduled. Point me at the list and the welcome-back flow can be live in September.
An SMS opt-in checkbox on the registration form
We capture mobile numbers already but record no consent, so today none of them are usable. One form field now makes SMS available for the February no-show push.
Conversion tracking on the registration form
Registration lives on Swapcard, a third-party domain, so we currently see clicks rather than registrations. Support enquiry is open; LinkedIn Lead Gen Forms is the fallback.
