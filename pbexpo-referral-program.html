The problem

Registration runs on Launchpad by Swapcard. No Swapcard product offers attendee referrals. Their "referral program" is for organizers referring other organizers. We looked at buying a dedicated tool and at building our own, and found a third option: a Launchpad feature we already have does the tracking.

The answer

Launchpad Marketing Codes. A code in the registration link (?_mc=REF00417) is saved on the registrant's record. We give every registrant their own code, so every referral is traceable back to the person who shared it.

How it works behind the scenes

Launchpad
Someone registersIf they came through a referral link, their record carries the referrer's code.
Zapier
Hand out the next codeA counter gives the new registrant the next unused code, e.g. registrant 417 gets REF00417.
HubSpot
Credit the referrerA workflow finds the contact who owns that code, links the two records and adds one to that person's referral count, but only if the new registrant has a qualifying job title.
HubSpot
Send the link and keep scoreThe new registrant gets their own link by email. A report sorted by referral count is the leaderboard.
Why pre-load the codes

Launchpad has to recognize a code before anyone uses it. Loading them all up front means nobody creates codes by hand as registrations come in. Each person still gets a code of their own.

The one catch

Tested: the code does not persist

If someone clicks a referral link, leaves, and comes back to register later without the link, the referral is lost. So the promotion has to tell people to register through the link itself.

Wording for every share message and landing page

Register using your colleague's link, not the homepage. If you leave and come back later, click the link again so they get credit.

Two safeguards

  • Links go straight to the registration form, not the PBExpo homepage. Fewer pages means fewer chances to wander off.
  • The "Who referred you?" field already on the registration form. If the link was not used but the referrer's code or name is there, marketing credits the referral by hand. The email asks people to enter the code, since it matches exactly.
