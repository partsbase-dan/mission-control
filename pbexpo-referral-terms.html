Official rules

To be published as Section 15 of the PBExpo terms and conditions, after Section 14.

15. PBExpo 2027 Referral Challenge

NO PURCHASE NECESSARY TO ENTER OR WIN. Registration for PBExpo 2027 is free.

15.1 Sponsor. PartsBase, Inc. ("PartsBase").

15.2 Eligibility. Open to individuals 18 years of age or older who hold a confirmed PBExpo 2027 registration. Not eligible: employees, officers, directors, agents and representatives of PartsBase, members of their immediate families or households, and employees of confirmed PBExpo 2027 exhibitors or sponsors. Void where prohibited or restricted by law.

15.3 Challenge Period. Begins January 5, 2027 at 12:01 a.m. ET and ends February 26, 2027 at 11:59 p.m. ET (the "Challenge Period"). PartsBase's records are the official timekeeper.

15.4 How to take part. Each eligible registrant receives a personal referral link and referral code by email. A referral is credited to you when another person completes a PBExpo 2027 registration during the Challenge Period, either (a) through your personal referral link, or (b) by entering your referral code in the "Who referred you?" field on the registration form. Registrations completed without your link or code cannot be credited. PartsBase is not responsible for referrals that cannot be tracked, including when a person leaves the registration page and returns later without the link.

15.5 Qualifying referrals. A referral counts only if the person referred:

  • is a new registrant who had not registered for PBExpo 2027 before the referral;
  • is a real, unique individual registering for themselves, with accurate information;
  • did not register with a Professional Level of "Student";
  • registered with a business email address. Registrations using a personal email service (for example Gmail, Yahoo, Outlook.com, Hotmail, iCloud or AOL) do not count;
  • is not you.

Each person can be credited to one referrer only. If a registration carries both a link and a different code, PartsBase decides which to credit, and that decision is final.

15.6 Prizes. Five (5) prizes will be awarded:

  • First place: one (1) PBExpo 2027 VIP wristband (Attendee + VIP Access), giving access to both VIP Hospitality Suites and the Airline & Operators Pavilion. Approximate retail value (ARV): $1,000 USD.
  • Second through fifth place: one (1) PBExpo 2027 Pavilion wristband (Attendee + Pavilion Access) each, giving access to the Airline & Operators Pavilion. ARV: $500 USD each.

Total ARV of all prizes: $3,000 USD. Prizes are registration upgrades for PBExpo 2027 only. Prizes are not transferable and have no cash value. No substitution will be made except at PartsBase's discretion. If a winner has already purchased the same upgrade, PartsBase will refund the amount the winner paid for that upgrade. Travel, accommodation and all other costs are the winner's responsibility.

15.7 How winners are determined. Winners are the five eligible participants with the most qualifying referrals at the end of the Challenge Period, ranked from first to fifth. No random drawing is used.

15.8 Ties. If two or more participants have the same number of qualifying referrals, the participant who reached that number first ranks higher. This is measured by the time their last qualifying referral was registered.

15.9 Verification and disqualification. Before awarding prizes, PartsBase will review each potential winner's referrals. PartsBase may remove any referral, or disqualify any participant, it reasonably believes involved false, duplicate or automated registrations, self-referral, or any other attempt to manipulate the results. Places are then re-ranked. PartsBase's decisions are final.

15.10 Winner notification and prize claim. Potential winners will be notified by email within five (5) business days after the Challenge Period ends. Winners must reply within seven (7) days of notification. Prizes are collected in person at PBExpo 2027. A winner who does not reply in time, is found ineligible, or does not attend forfeits the prize, and the next-ranked eligible participant may be awarded it.

15.11 Taxes. Winners are responsible for all federal, state and local taxes on their prize.

15.12 Your information. Referral activity is handled under the PBExpo Privacy Notice and Section 3. Taking part does not, by itself, sign you or the people you refer up for additional marketing communications.

15.13 General. Section 12 (Governing Law) and the dispute terms in Section 14 apply to this Challenge. PartsBase may modify, suspend or end the Challenge if it cannot run as planned, including because of fraud or technical failure.

15.14 Winner list. For the names of the winners, email info@pbexpo.org by April 30, 2027.
