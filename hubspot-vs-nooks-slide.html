Test HubSpot sequences first — then decide on Nooks
Prepared for Robert
Martech / RevOps
Advise:
Wait & Test

Have Sales leaders validate outbound with HubSpot sequences before buying an AI SDR tool. Kevin is live on HubSpot sequences within 2 weeks — that gives us a real baseline first.

HubSpot covers existing contacts, form fills, and event attendees. Nooks is a strong fit for net-new prospects we don't want to load into HubSpot — but only once the foundation below is solid.

HubSpot vs. Nooks — where each fits
Dimension HubSpot (have it) Nooks (AI SDR)
Best for Existing contacts, form fills, event / demo leads Net-new outbound at volume; parallel dialing
Core job CRM + sequences, nurture, campaign logic AI dialer, prospecting, call automation
Data needs Works with what we have today Requires very complete, clean data to perform
Campaign logic Visualizes who should / shouldn't get outreach Assumes targeting is already defined
Status Onboarding now — Kevin live in ~2 wks Evaluate after baseline test
Why not buy right now — 3 reasons
1
Data isn't clean enough yet
AI SDR tools rely on complete data. Ours needs meaningful cleanup before Nooks can perform.
2
No clear campaign logic yet
Marketing vs. sales campaign logic isn't defined. HubSpot is where teams see why a person should — or shouldn't — get an email.
3
ICPs still being finalized by unit
PartStore, PBExpo & PB each need defined targets. Without them, teams could work leads another unit owns.
Bottom line: HubSpot for existing + inbound now. Revisit Nooks for net-new once data, campaign logic, and ICPs are in place.
