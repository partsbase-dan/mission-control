Choose one of the templates below. For each blank, type in your own words — you'll see exactly what to write and where it ends up. When you're done, click Download to save the finished email to your computer, ready to upload to HubSpot.

Trying an alternate layout of this tool? Compare with the live-preview version or the original.
💡 About the images: this tool can't upload pictures for you. First upload each image to HubSpot (Marketing → Files and Templates → Files), then copy the link HubSpot gives you and paste it into the matching box below. Any box you leave empty keeps the template's current content — so it's safe to only fill in the parts you want to change.
